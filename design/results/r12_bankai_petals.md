## What was done

### 1. The sword built from petals (shikai and bankai)
* **Film:** the release dissolve (`ShikaiDissolve` look: the blade cut into fragments, tip first leaving, spiral flight, pink glow shells) is played **backwards** by the item model at `el = total * (1 - form)` (`ObjItemBakedModel#emitSwordForm`, reuses `emitFragment`, `emitBloomLayers` and the flight code, which was split out as `emitShardFlight`). Form rising: fragments fly in and join the pink glowing blade from the hilt to the tip; form falling: the same film forwards (the blade glows pink again and breaks up). Texture: the normal drawn blade of the sealed state, tinted pink (`release.pinkColor`, `release.glowColor` like the release), so "the same texture, only pink and glowing".
* **After the build:** `pink` eases from 1 to 0 over `petalBlade.settle` (0.45 s): the pink blade turns into the normal one (the whole sealed blade mesh, own colours). Before the break up the pink returns during `petalBlade.lead` (0.25 s).
* **Shikai:** the shikai hilt stays (as now), only the blade is built. **Bankai:** the hilt and the guard (the part of the sealed drawn sword behind the saya mouth) appear pink from a third of the film, then turn normal, with the blade; so it is the whole sword. The hand item of the bankai is empty without a strike, as before.
* **Pose:** `drawsInHand()` is true for the bankai while the local player's sword is at least 12 percent built (`PetalBladeFx.localFormed`), so `FirstPersonHand`, `FpMotion`, `FpCast` hold and swing the arm like with a sword (arm pose = the item default `first_person_arm`). Third person already poses the arms for any zanpakuto in the main hand (`TpPoseDriver`, `BcItemPose`), so nothing changed there. UNVERIFIED in both views.
* **Inflying petals:** at the start of a build 36 pink petals (`FxParticles` PETAL, same as the melee trail) fly in from a ball of radius 1.1 to 1.9 around the hand point (`petalBlade.petals`), visible from the third person.
* **Clock (`PetalBladeFx`, `PetalBladeMath.Timeline`):** triggers as in round 1 (Better Combat `ATTACK_START` for the local player, the start of the vanilla swing for other players, `ZanpakutoItem.isBlocking` polled every tick). Mode B: the first strike or block builds, it holds `petalBlade.hold` (3.0) seconds from the hit moment, each strike or block restarts it. Mode A (`petalBlade.mode` 0): the sword lives for the strike (`holdA` 0.25 s after the sweep, and while blocking). A strike during the break up pulls the film back from where it is (no jump).
* Timing: build up `petalBlade.assemble` 0.55 s (the film is 1.13 s, so it runs about twice as fast as the release, to be there at the hit), break up `petalBlade.disperse` 0.9 s.
* Not done: the **world petal part** of the release effect (`ShikaiDissolve.draw`: petal cards, halos, sparks along the blade) is not run backwards; the flying fragments and glow shells are in the item model, the inflying petals are the particles above. If it looks poor next to the release, that is the missing piece.

### 2. Third person swarm: figure eight / Moebius loops (`SwarmMath.ribbonIdle`, `RibbonParams`)
* Replaced the spiral ribbons (a cylinder) by 3 closed loops: a circle of radius 0.95 around the body with a vertical weave of twice the frequency (amplitude 0.55): seen from the side that is a figure eight. The loops are tilted against each other (0.40 rad, alternating sign), the whole pattern turns slowly (0.1 rev per s). The petals sit in a flat band (0.14 wide, 0.04 thick) that turns by half a turn per loop (`rbTwist` 0.5): a Moebius strip. Closed loops: nothing shrinks at an end any more, the lag behind a moving owner is strongest on the two lobes.
* From R12 round 1 and kept: half of the petals (`swarm.tpKeep` 0.5), glow x0.4 (`swarm.tpGlow`), the swarm clears out of the way of the sword while the owner strikes (`swarm.swordClear`, `swordReach`, swing clock of `PetalBladeFx`). Measured (simulated camera 3.5 blocks behind): before R12 spiral: 999 petals, 132 in the sword zone, 60 glints, 63 halos, mean radius 1.09; now: 500 petals idle (420 striking), 0 in the sword zone while striking, 32 glints and 29 halos (their intensity is also x0.4), mean radius 0.94. Tests: `SwarmMathTest` (closed loops cross the middle height at least 4 times per turn, stay 0.45 to 1.7 from the axis, the radius varies with the height so it is not a cylinder).

## Round 4 (glow slid off the blade, bankai sword not pink enough; build green, 1389 tests, 0 failures)
1. **Glow off the blade, cause found from the screenshot numbers:** the first person hand is drawn with a fixed fov of 70 degrees (vanilla `getFov(..., false)` ignores the option), the world with the fov of the options. The captured hand pose was used as a world position without that. With the crosshair as the screen centre (180, 500) the real sword root (370, 700) and tip (470, 20) times 0.62 land on (298, 624) and (360, 202), where the glow was seen ((290, 630), (350, 210)); 0.62 = 1 / tan(F/2)/tan(35 deg) for F about 96.6 degrees. Fix: `ShikaiDissolve.toWorld` scales the offset across the view by `HandFov.scale(fov)` for the owner's first person (`core/fx/HandFov`, `HandFovTest` incl. the numbers above). It fixes the glow of the petal sword in shikai and bankai and also the world part of the release dissolve (it had the same offset for any fov other than 70). Keys: `fx.handFovFix` 1 (0 = off), `fx.handFov` (0 = the option; set it if you sprint a lot, the sprint fov change is not included). UNVERIFIED in the game.
2. **Bankai sword fully pink (blade, guard, hilt):** over the normal sword the same quads are drawn again pushed out by 0.004, emissive, every corner on one texel of the pink petal texture, so no steel or copper shows (`ObjItemBakedModel#flatPink`, `ObjItemUnbakedModel.BakedStates.pinkSprite`), alpha `pink * petalBlade.shell` up to 0.97; in the bankai it stays, in the shikai the blade overlay fades out with the pink (1.45 s) and the blade turns back into the normal steel. The guard and hilt overlay is bankai only. Together with the white hot core and pink edge of the world glow it is the anime sword; the flat pink loses the shading of the blade mesh. `petalBlade.shell` lowers it (0 = no overlay).

## Round 5 (glow still slid off the blade when moving; build green, 1389 tests, 0 failures)

Rather than reproduce the whole view matrix chain, **in the owner's first person the glow is now made of the item model** (`ObjItemBakedModel#emitFlatBloom`: three flat pink emissive shells of the blade fragments pushed out from the axis, 1.6 times the release bloom width), so it sits on the blade whatever the camera does. The world glow (`PetalBladeFx.emitGlow`) is skipped in the owner's first person (`petalBlade.fpWorldGlow` 1 brings it back); in third person it stays (positions from the pose with the camera rotation; the camera bobbing of the third person view is not corrected, UNVERIFIED). Keys: `petalBlade.bloom` (strength, 0 = no shells), `petalBlade.bloomWidth` 1.6, `petalBlade.fpWorldGlow` 0. The glow is now halo shells and the pink overlay, without the white hot core streak in first person.

## Round 6 (glow off in third person, uneven glow along the blade, glow off when running at the shikai release; build green, 1390 tests, 0 failures)

2. **Uneven glow (more at the bottom, a bulb at the tip):** the shells were pushed out from the blade axis, which is undefined for the fragments on the flat faces and swells the tip. 3. **Glow slides off when running at the shikai activation:** the old release effect (`ShikaiDissolve.emitGlow`) places the halo along the blade, the hot line, the hilt light and the flashes on the front in the world from the captured hand pose, so running (view bobbing, hurt tilt) moved them. The world petals of the dissolve stay. The fov correction of round 4 now uses the projection of the frame (`1 / m11`), so the sprint fov is included. UNVERIFIED: the world petals of the release dissolve (placed from shard positions of the same pose) may still be a few centimetres off when running.

## Round 7 (dark stripes and a second sword offset from the blade; build green, 1390 tests, 0 failures)
Cause (screenshot 6, by reading the geometry): the normals of the sealed sword mesh are not consistent, on some faces they point into the blade. The pink overlay (pushed out by 0.004 along the normal) and the glow shells (pushed along the normal in round 6) went **behind** the steel on those faces, so the dark steel showed through as stripes and the pushed copy of the guard and the blade looked like a second sword beside the first. Fix: the pink overlay now lies exactly on the steel (push 0; the translucent layer passes the depth test at equal depth), and the glow shells are the blade fragments **widened about the blade axis** (offset of every corner from the axis x (1 + k), k = 0.6 / 1.3 / 2.4, `petalBlade.bloomWidth` scales k) instead of pushed along a normal; the tip stays a point. No surface normal is used for any offset any more. UNVERIFIED in the game.

## Round 9 (the slabs at the guard were still there, also at the shikai release; build green, 1390 tests, 0 failures)
Measured on the mesh (`byakuya_sealed_drawn.obj`, y-up file space, probe scripts in the scratchpad): the blade axis the code uses (saya mouth (0, 0.257, 0) to the tip (0, 0.9785, -0.0864)) is **not the centre line of the blade**. The root of the blade and the guard plate stand 0.056 m off it (guard fragments at rc 0.051 to 0.066, x +-0.038, z about -0.055), the middle of the blade 0.028 m (median), the tip 0 (the blade is curved, the chord misses the root). So every effect that grows or pushes the glow shells about that axis (round 6/7/8 flat bloom, and the old release bloom `emitBloomLayers`, which pushes by the unit vector from the axis) displaced the blade shells sideways and stretched the guard fragments into wide flat slabs (the 2 to 3 stacked slabs per side = the three shell layers). Round 8's cut by distance from the axis could not work (guard fragments sit at 0.051 to 0.066, the cut was 0.059).
Fix: **each fragment is grown about its own centre** (1 + k, k = 0.6 / 1.3 / 2.4, `petalBlade.bloomWidth` scales k), no axis and no normal anywhere; a fragment is a few centimetres, so the halo is a few centimetres everywhere, also at the guard. The distance-from-axis code of round 8 was removed. The same flat pink shells are now also the glow of the **release hold and dissolve** (`emitBloomAuto`; the old layers only when the item has no pink texture), so entering the shikai shows the same look and no slabs. UNVERIFIED in the game. The axis (`bladeBase`, `bladeDir`) is still used for the order of the dissolve and to split blade and hilt, where the small error does not matter.

## Round 10 (noise on the blade, a flat bar at the guard; build green, 1390 tests, 0 failures)
1. **Noise on the blade:** the glow shells lie in the plane of the steel (growing a planar fragment about its own centre keeps it in that plane), so their depth differed from the steel only by rounding, pixel by pixel. Now each shell is pushed a little along the OUTWARD normal (0.0015 / 0.004 / 0.008 model units x `petalBlade.bloomWidth`), with the outward side computed per fragment (away from the mean of the fragments of its slice of the blade, `BladeShards.nsign`, because the normals of the file are not consistent). The in-plane growth is smaller now (k 0.5 / 1.0 / 1.8). When the pink is complete (the whole bankai sword, `pink * petalBlade.shell` at 1) the flat pink layer is opaque and the steel under it is **not drawn at all** (blade, hilt, and the steel fragments of the film), so two textures no longer overlap there; the shikai blade still cross-fades with the steel while the pink fades out.
2. **The flat bar at the guard:** the guard plate is cut into long thin fragments and their shells grew into a bar past the guard. Measured on the mesh the guard is at s 0 to 0.009 along the blade axis and the blade starts at about 0.05, so fragments with s < 0.03 get no shell now (`BLOOM_MIN_S`). The flat pink layer of the guard itself stays. UNVERIFIED in the game.

## Round 11 (lines across the blade in shikai and bankai; build green, 1390 tests, 0 failures)
Cause: the glow shells were copies of the blade fragments (each blade quad is cut into 3 x 2 tiles), grown about their own centres, so neighbouring tiles overlapped and the overlaps (stacked translucent layers) showed as bright bands across the blade at every tile boundary. **The fragment shells are gone** (`emitFlatBloom` removed). The glow is now made of whole strips along the real centre line of the blade (the mean of the fragment centres per slice, baked in `BladeShards.clp/cls`): three nested margins, each two crossed hollow strips, one beside the flat of the blade and one beside its thickness, starting at the edge of the blade (`BLADE_HALF_Z` 0.0165, `BLADE_HALF_X` 0.0055, measured on the mesh: the blade is 0.032 wide along z and 0.010 thick along x, tapers about a third, and its centre line leaves the chord by up to 0.028) and reaching 1.0 / 2.2 / 3.8 cm beyond it (`petalBlade.bloomWidth` scales the reach), alpha 0.32 / 0.19 / 0.10 x strength. They never lie over the blade and never overlap neighbours, so there is nothing to show as a line; the film (build up, break up, release hold and dissolve) uses them limited to the part of the blade the front has not reached. The flat pink layer on the blade, the guard and the hilt is unchanged. UNVERIFIED in the game. If something is still drawn as lines, the remaining candidates are the flat overlay quads themselves (each original quad once, no overlap) and the steel under them when the pink is not complete (shikai while it fades).
