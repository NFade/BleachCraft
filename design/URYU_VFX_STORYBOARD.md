# URYU VFX STORYBOARD: frame-by-frame effects, poses, models and the U-FX implementation contract

Status: **DRAFT v1 for implementation** (visual direction prepared from canon references, 2026-10-14, branch `gin-uryu`). Nothing here was rendered or run: every number is a starting value for the harness (section 7 lists what is UNVERIFIED). This file is the visual contract for Uryu Ishida the way `VFX_STORYBOARD.md` is for Rukia and Byakuya, and it uses the same format, abbreviations and quality tiers (read `VFX_STORYBOARD.md` sections 1, 8, 10, 11 first; they are not repeated).
Binding order (later wins): `URYU_PLAN.md` revision 4 (gameplay) < `URYU.md` (canon facts) < `VFX_STORYBOARD.md` (toolbox) < this file for everything visual and audible of Uryu. Gameplay numbers are NEVER changed here; where a visual needs new server data it is listed as **S-U1 .. S-U4** in section 5.
Replaces the placeholders of `URYU_PLAN.md` section 6 "U-FX pending" (`UryuFx`, `UryuArrowRenderer`, `UryuSeeleRenderer`, `UryuBowPose`, the plain item bow). Related: `results/uryu_u4_u5.md`, `results/uryu_round2.md`, `results/gin_uryu_models.md`.

Abbreviations (additions to `VFX_STORYBOARD.md`): **P:RM** `reishi_mote` particle, **P:FL** `flechette` flake particle (new types, 3.3); **G:x** glow sprite x, from the existing atlas (`ring_thin`, `ring_soft`, `glow_soft`, `glow_core`, `star4`, `star6`, `streak`, `line`, `speck`, `flare`) or from the NEW Quincy atlas `uryu_glow` (names in 4.3: `zeichen_star`, `web_wheel`, `burst_rays`, `crescent`, `platform_disc`, `zeichen_cross`, `pent_line`, `chevrons`, `diamond_star`, `flake`, `needle`, `vein_trace`, `glitter`, `dash`, `lens`, `flechette_trail`, `beam_glitter`, `rune_band`, `star_field`, `fence`); **M:x** instanced mesh of the new set `uryu_fx` (4.1) or of the existing `uryu` set; **Q:x** additive strip/beam/wall of `FxShapes`; **D:x** ground decal (`FxDecals`); **ZK** = Quincy Zeichen (the pentagram with a ring at each point, 0.3). Times in seconds from the packet that starts the effect (`t = 0`), ticks written as "N t". Easing names as in VFX_STORYBOARD (`oc`, `ob`, `oe`, `iq`, `ios`).

**Canon rules that change the placeholders (decided):**
1. The Sprenger ground figure is a **pentagon outline with five spokes and a hub**, and a ZK pentagram (star with rings) is the SEAL glyph that flares at the explosion (both exist in canon, [U sprenger 4], [U quincy_zeichen 2]). The placeholder draws only a star.
2. Arrows are **needles with a chevron fletching and a star-sparkle head**, trails are thin and white-cored; no fat comet.
3. Wings are **angular crystal bars with a stardust texture**, not a fan of feathers; **bands on forearms and boots, a chevron emblem on the chest**, loose flake flechettes in the air; no halo.
4. Impact = star sparkle plus **burst rays plus white dust puffs** (Licht Regen, Sprenger), never a plain explosion sprite.
5. Everything is cyan on dark and white at the core: the mod has to **darken the world for Quincy moments** (GRADE) so the neon reads.

### 1.2 What glows, what is solid
- **Additive only (glow layer, no depth write):** bow outline layer, string, arrow glow, glyphs on the ground and in the air, veins, beams, flechette flakes, stardust, impact flashes, the wing overglow pass.
- **Alpha + emissive translucent (reads at noon):** the wing plates (alpha 200, emissive map), the arrow mesh, the bow mesh, the bracer and boot bands, the Sprenger fence curtain (alpha 90), ground scar decals, dust puffs. Rule 5 of the storyboard (nothing may vanish on a bright sky) holds: every hero shape has an alpha component.
- **Never glows:** the player skin, the coat, items other than the bow, the blade of the Seele Schneider handle sleeve.

### 1.3 Visual language (what makes it Quincy, not Rukia, not Byakuya)
- **Rukia** is soft: pale desaturated ice, snow, mist, rounded crystals, white silence. **Byakuya** is warm and fluid: pink petals, silver slabs, moonlight. **Uryu** is *hard-edged and electric*: saturated cyan outlines with a white core, straight lines and sharp angles, facets, tiny glitter. Motion: **snap and radiate** (`oe` bursts, straight-line travel, radial lines), no drifting clouds except the stardust.
- **Sacred geometry:** (a) the ZK pentagram with rings at the points and a dashed pentagon behind it; (b) the Quincy cross: circle with four angled arms (`zeichen_cross`), used as the sign that appears at the nock at full Focus and behind the back in the bankai; (c) the spoked wheel (`web_wheel`) that opens the bow; (d) the chevron stack (`chevrons`) on the chest and the arrow fletching; (e) the flechette flake (leaf with comb barbs) as the particle, the barrage arrow and the giant arrow's segments; (f) the four-point diamond star (`diamond_star`) as the head of the giant arrow.
- **Stardust:** every Quincy moment carries sparse glitter specks (`speck`, `glitter`, `star_field`): "stars" are the signature of Sternenstaub and the wings.
- **Grade:** Quincy moments are lit by darkening the world (VFX rule 3): GRADE `#6F88C8` (s 0.5 to 0.7) for the bankai release, `#3C4F8A` (s 0.5) for Sternenstaub, always faded out.
- **Ground language:** pentagon outline, ZK, platform discs, ring of dust.

## 2. Effects (frame-by-frame)
All effects are drawn on the client from `effect_event` ids 70 to 77, the data packets of `UryuArrowsS2C` (ARROWS, IMPACT, BURST, LINE, TRAP, SLASH, FOCUS) and the synced attachment (state), never from guesses. `UryuFx.handles` today accepts 72 to 77 only: **70 and 71 (the releases) must be added** (U-FX 6, 7). Server timings (ticks) are those of `UryuBalance` and `BalanceConfig`. Screen values are multiplied by `screenFxScale`; sounds are vanilla (verified ids of VFX_STORYBOARD 1.5; ids marked with a dagger are known vanilla ids of 1.21.1 that must be confirmed by the same `SoundEvents.java` check in U-FX 0: `entity.arrow.shoot`, `entity.arrow.hit`, `item.crossbow.shoot`, `entity.firework_rocket.twinkle`, `entity.firework_rocket.blast`, `entity.firework_rocket.launch`, `entity.experience_orb.pickup`).

Anchors used: **hand** = the bow hand (right hand of the owner for a right hander as built, see 7.2): third person `feet + R(bodyYaw) * (0.35 right, 1.10 up, 0.45 fwd)`; first person `camera + (0.30 right, -0.28 up, 0.55 fwd)` in camera space. **nock** = the draw point of the string (model empty `bow_string_top`/`bottom` midpoint pulled back, 2.2). **tips** = `UryuBalance.WING_TIPS` relative to the feet (upper (+-1.7, 2.35, 0.45), lower (+-1.25, 1.2, 0.6), rotated by body yaw).

### 3.1 Representation (the existing pose docs)
- **Third person (TP)** follows `design/anim/player_game_rig.bbmodel`, the FLAT game rig of `tools/bbmodel_to_playeranim.py`: groups `head, torso, rightArm, leftArm, rightLeg, leftLeg` (all children of the root; pivots torso/head (0,24,0), arms (+-5,22,0), legs (+-1.9,12,0) in Blockbench px), degrees `[rx, ry, rz]`, model faces -Z, own right is +X. Verified meaning from the exporter and `design/anim/block_poses_keyframes.json`: **rx 0 = arm hanging, +90 = arm horizontal forward, +180 = straight up** (`pitch = -rx`); **ry positive moves the tip of the right arm toward the body centre (the left arm: negative)**; **rz positive swings the arm outward** (right arm +X) (`roll = +rz`); torso `ry` positive turns the chest to the model's left; position offsets in px; the item group `rightItem` carries the held bow in GAME item-frame degrees (`uryu_cross_display.json` solved values: idle `[-118, 0, -180]`, draw `[-180, 5.73, -180]`, `UryuBowPose`). Time is in ticks (20/s), easing names of the exporter (`OUTCUBIC`, `INOUTSINE`, `OUTBACK` ...).
- **First person (FP)** uses the `FpCast` rows of `design/results/fp_cast_rows.json`: `R [t, px, py, pz, rx, ry, rz, ease]` (the arm holding the item with the item as one body; blocks in the hand frame, x right, y up, z forward = negative; degrees, `rx` < 0 tips the item forward-down), `L [t, px, py, pz, arx, ary, arz, free, ease]` (the free hand, `free` 1 = the fist stays in the stance frame independent of the item), `C [t, pitch, yaw, roll, fov, ease]` (camera, pitch > 0 up), `TR/TL [t, pitch, yaw, roll, ease]` (third person arm of the same gesture; pitch < 0 raises forward, roll > 0 outward for the right arm). Ease: 0 smooth, 1 out, 2 in, 3 linear, 4 spring. **All values are deltas from the rest hold.** For Uryu the rest hold is the vanilla bow hold plus the display transform (there is no sword stance: `FpMotion.handModel` returns null for Uryu); the new class `UryuBowMotion` (U-FX 11) supplies the rest frame and adds these rows; the rows replace `NewCharacterGestures` entries `uryu.*`.

Canon holds the bow in the LEFT hand and draws with the right: mirror rule = swap `rightArm`/`leftArm` and negate `ry`, `rz` and the torso `ry` (left handers already mirror in code).
