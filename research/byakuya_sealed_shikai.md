## 2. Appearance (per state)

### 2a. Sealed (katana at rest)
- Overall length: not stated on Fandom. Estimate about 100 cm, `confidence: low`. Basis: Fandom says the sealed sword "resembles a typical katana"; Byakuya is 180 cm tall (Fandom infobox).
- Blade length: not stated. Estimate about 70 cm, `confidence: low`.
- Blade curvature: standard katana curve, `confidence: med` (sealed stills show a normal katana profile, e.g. byakuya_sealed_3, byakuya_sealed_2).
- Tsuba (guard): bronze, open crossguard with a frame like a four-pane window ([Fandom](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Byakuya_Kuchiki), high). Manga colour panel (byakuya_sealed_6) shows the bronze open frame; sampled colour `#A58D5F`, `confidence: med` (estimate from image).
- Tsuka (hilt) and tsuka-ito (wrap): hilt fittings are bronze, and the hilt is laced with lavender fabric cord ([Fandom](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Byakuya_Kuchiki), high). Lavender wrap sampled from the anime full-length art (byakuya_sealed_1): `#BCB2D3`, `confidence: med` (estimate from image). Wrap pattern: fine diagonal cord, `confidence: med` (visible in byakuya_sealed_1 and byakuya_sealed_2).
- Kashira (pommel), menuki: not stated on Fandom. Not checked beyond the stills.
- Saya (scabbard): white ([Fandom](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Byakuya_Kuchiki), high). Note: in the stills where the scabbard is visible at the hip (byakuya_sealed_4, byakuya_sealed_3), it reads dark or purple rather than white. Unresolved, see section 8.
- Ribbons, tassels, sashes: none stated. The white obi sash is clothing, not part of the sword.
- Glow / emissive parts: none. The sealed sword does not glow.
- Colours (HEX estimates):
  - Bronze tsuba and bronze hilt fittings: `#A58D5F` (manga panel byakuya_sealed_6, estimate)
  - Lavender hilt cord: `#BCB2D3` (anime art byakuya_sealed_1, estimate)
  - White saya: `#F5F5F2` (Fandom colour word "white", estimate of tone)
  - Blade steel (drawn, unreleased): `#C9CED6` (estimate, no source)

### 2b. Shikai (released)
- Blade form: the blade separates into "a thousand" tiny, slender blades that fly away from the handle; the handle stays in Byakuya's hand ([Fandom](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Byakuya_Kuchiki), high).
- Shard count: "a thousand" (Fandom, high). Individual shard count not given.
- Shard size: not stated. Fandom says the blades are "too small and swift to be seen". Estimate a few millimetres to about 1 cm, `confidence: low`.
- Shard colour (anime): pink. Manga: white. Fandom Trivia: "In the manga, Senbonzakura's petals are white, while in the anime they are pink." (high). Sampled from the anime petal dome (byakuya_shikai_3): body `#E5A4DC`, highlight `#F9C8F6`, `confidence: med` (estimate from image; bankai-era frames not used for this sample). Sampled from byakuya_shikai_6 (petal bloom): `#FEA2D3`, `confidence: med`.
- Manga petal colour: white, estimate `#F5F5F5`, `confidence: med` (Fandom states white; no manga petal image downloaded).
- Glow: none emitted. Fandom says the blades reflect light, so they look like cherry blossom petals (high). Glow seen in some stills is the attack effect around the blades, not the blades themselves.
- Hilt in hand: only the handle and tsuba remain (see section 4c).

## 3. Transition between states
- Sealed to shikai: Byakuya speaks "Chire" (Scatter). The blade separates into thousands of tiny blades, which fly out and leave the handle in his hand (Fandom, high). Duration, light and camera timing: not stated on Fandom. Open.
- Release can be stopped before it finishes. Fandom lists two cases:
  - Yoruichi wraps the sealed blade in cloth before it separates, during Byakuya's second fight with Ichigo ([Fandom](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Byakuya_Kuchiki), "Release Negation", high). Still: byakuya_sealed_7 (Ep41, file name "Ep41YoruichiStopsByakuya").
  - Renji strikes the sealed blade with Zabimaru before the release ([Fandom](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Byakuya_Kuchiki), high).
- The release can be overwhelmed by a stronger concussive force. Fandom: Ichigo's Getsuga Tenshō disperses the blades during their final duel (high).
- Anime vs manga transition differences: Fandom names the petal colour difference (see section 2b). No other transition difference is stated. Open.

## 4. Abilities

### 4a. Shikai: swarm control
- **Name:** Shikai release "Chire" (散れ). No separate technique name for the shikai on Fandom. kanji / romaji / English sub / English dub: 散れ / Chire / Scatter / Scatter (dub not separately verified). Source: [Fandom](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Byakuya_Kuchiki), high.
- Effect: the blades are steered by swinging the handle. Fandom's shikai special ability: "By swinging the handle, Byakuya controls the scattered blades" (high). The handle is the control device; no mental or hand control is listed for the shikai.
- Hand speed claim: RESOLVED. Fandom places the "using his hands doubles speed" claim under the Bankai section ("guiding them with his hands increases their speed twofold"). The shikai section does not mention it. The Wikipedia shikai claim (mental control plus twice the speed by hand) conflicts and is treated as an error (see section 8). Confidence in the bankai attribution: high.
- Visuals: shards pink in the anime (estimate `#E5A4DC` body, `#F9C8F6` highlight); white in the manga. Count "a thousand". Size estimate about 1 cm, low.
- Scale: arena scale. Shards strike from distance and can destroy structures (Fandom). Exact radius not stated.
- Duration: not stated.
- Sound character: not stated.
- Command phrase spoken by user: "Chire" (散れ).
- First appearance: Byakuya's manga debut is Volume 6, Chapter 51, and his anime debut is Episode 15 (Fandom infobox, high). The earliest shikai release in the Fandom Plot is the Ganju Shiba scene (Plot: Byakuya releases the shikai and it shreds Ganju's body). Chapter and episode number for that scene are not on Fandom. Open.

### 4b. Shikai: attack and defence
- Attack (Fandom, high): the blades "shred opponents from a distance, destroy structures and break through almost any defense". Early in the fight with Äs Nödt and Mask De Masculine the shikai dug a hole deep into the ground under the enemy.
- Attack after training at the Soul King Palace (Fandom, high): Byakuya forms numerous huge conic constructs with the shikai blades to destroy a multi-directional swarm attack from Äs Nödt's Vollständig form. The sword shows greater mass and attack power after the training, and Äs mistook it for the bankai (Fandom Plot).
- Defence (Fandom, high): the blades ward off blows from hand-to-hand fighters and swordsmen, and wipe out energy projectiles such as Reishi thorns, by forming a defensive wall.
- Dome / full enclosure (Fandom, high): after the training, the blades can fully enclose Byakuya to protect him from shockwaves, for example the one from closing the portal to the Soul King Palace. Fandom does not use the word "dome" for the shikai; it describes full enclosure. No radius is given.
- Hurtless Area (Fandom, high): Mushōken 無傷圏 ("Woundless Zone" in the literal translation; "Safe Zone" in the Viz translation). The blades do not strike anything within 85 cm of Byakuya unless he lets them in. The Hurtless Area can be exploited (Tsukishima's trick: moving close, Fandom "Hurtless Area Complication").
- Visuals: as in 2b. Swarm colour pink (anime); conic constructs and walls take the same petal look (estimate). Scale: 85 cm safe radius (high).

### 4c. Hilt left in hand after release
- After release only the handle and tsuba stay in Byakuya's hand (Fandom, high). The handle is then swung to steer the blades (Fandom, high). Fandom says the bankai reconstitutes the sealed sword in his hand when the bankai ends (not shikai).

### 4d. Named shikai techniques
- None. Fandom's shikai section names no techniques. Named techniques (Gōkei, Senkei, Ikka Senjinka, Shūkei: Hakuteiken) all belong to the bankai and are out of scope here (Fandom, high).

## 5. Anime vs manga differences
- Petal colour: manga petals are white; anime petals are pink (Fandom Trivia, high).
- Hilt display: Fandom Trivia says that in the anime, Byakuya is wrongly shown with the Senbonzakura hilt during Zommari's fight when the bankai is deactivated. Not a shikai difference (bankai context, high).
- Anime-only shikai techniques: none listed (high, based on Fandom section 15).
- Renji impalement scene: Fandom Trivia says the manga and anime differ in how Renji's arm is impaled by the Senkei-like swords (bankai context, not shikai, high).

## 8. Open questions / uncertainty
1. Shikai chapter and episode numbers: Fandom text gives chapter and episode numbers only for the bankai (Shūkei: ch. 166 / ep. 59, for example). Shikai numbers come from wiki file names (Ep41, Ep52, Ep385) and are med confidence. First shikai scene (Ganju) has no number. Open.
2. Hand control (resolved): Wikipedia's shikai claim (mental control, twice the speed by hand) conflicts with Fandom, which places the hand-speed claim under the bankai. Fandom is treated as correct.
3. Saya colour: Fandom says white. The sheath in stills 3, 4 (sealed_3, sealed_4) reads dark or purple. Could be lighting, or the scabbard has a dark finish. Unresolved; check a manga or anime still where the scabbard is isolated.
4. Blade and overall length: not stated on Fandom. Estimates in section 2 are low confidence.
5. Shard size and shard colour per count: not stated. Only "a thousand" and "too small to see" are given. The HEX values are estimates from the anime frames.
6. Glow: Fandom says the blades reflect light. No emission is stated. The bright halo in some frames is effect art.
7. Shikai state unconfirmed for images 4 and 8 (section 7b). Bankai-era: images 5 and 6. Reigai: image 7.
8. Manga petal colour (white) is from Fandom text. No manga petal image was used; the manga sample (byakuya_sealed_6) shows only the blade.
9. Russian transcription (Сэнбонсакура / Чире) is mine, not sourced.
10. Wikipedia's shikai dub note ("die" in volume 14) is not on Fandom. Keep "Scatter" as the English sub.
