Spelling notes:
- No macrons in these names. "Togame" is the romaji used by the wiki; one search summary reported a variant spelling "Hakka no Togane" (likely a typo) and a Japanese blog used "白霞の祟り" (White Haze's curse), which is not the official name. Use "Hakka no Togame".
- Official English translation (VIZ) is "White Haze Punishment"; game/wiki literal is "Censure of the White Haze". Pick one for the mod's text and note the choice.
- Russian transcription (for voice matching): zanpakuto "Содэ но Ширайуки" or "Сёдэ но Сирайки"; bankai "Хакка но Тогамэ" or "Хакка но Тогаме".

## 2. Appearance (bankai)
- Overall length, blade length, shape: no measurement in the wiki. Estimate only: body height 144 cm ([src1](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Rukia_Kuchiki), character profile). Blade is described as still resembling the shikai form, which is a normal katana when sealed (see shikai file). Estimate overall ~100 cm, blade ~70 cm, straight-ish katana shape. confidence: low.
- Tsuba (guard): not described in the bankai state. Shikai tsuba becomes a hollow snowflake-like circle ([src1]). Bankai tsuba: unknown, confidence: low.
- Tsuka and tsuka-ito: not described in bankai. Sealed hilt is reddish-brown ([src1]). Confidence: low.
- Kashira and menuki: not described. Confidence: low.
- Saya (scabbard): not described in bankai. Confidence: low.
- Blade: transparent ice, still resembling its shikai form ([src1]). Confidence: high (wiki text).
- Ribbons: long, flowing ribbons tied at her back, forming numerous large loops ([src1]). Count: "numerous" (not counted). Colour: white (wiki implies white). Confidence: med.
- Clothing: white, ankle-length kimono with lined patterns; ornate collar, edges and shoulder design made of transparent ice; long, wide sleeves ([src1]). Confidence: high (wiki text).
- Ice flower: a small ice flower formation at the centre of her chest ([src1]). Confidence: high.
- Half crown: ice crown extending around the back of her head ([src1]). Confidence: high.
- Hair and irises: become white ([src1]). Confidence: high.
- Ōken clothing: destroyed when she activates bankai, which gives her temporary new clothing ([src1], ch. 570 p. 7). Confidence: med.

Colours (HEX estimates, visually estimated from the anime ep. 385 still rukia_bankai_3, the manga ch. 570 colour page rukia_bankai_2 and the Ep. 385 Äs Nödt freeze frame rukia_bankai_5; wiki text where the images do not show the part):

## 3. Transition between states
- Activation (manga ch. 569 p. 1-16, anime ep. 385): a blinding white pillar appears when she releases bankai ([src1]). Confidence: high (wiki text).
- A pillar of cold mist rises into the air from her position at release ([src1], ch. 569 p. 16). Confidence: high.
- After freezing Äs Nödt solid, a crack appears on the back of her hand while she stands in bankai form ([src1], ch. 569 end). Confidence: med.
- Clothing: the ice kimono and hair/eye colour change happen on release; her Ōken clothing is destroyed ([src1], ch. 570 p. 7). Confidence: med.
- Release back (de-activation): she must bring her body temperature back up slowly, or she risks harming herself. Byakuya tells her to slow down while returning to normal temperature ([src1], ch. 570 p. 4-10). Confidence: high.
- Duration of activation: not stated in the wiki. Confidence: low.
- Anime (ep. 385): visuals are the same design in the wiki images; specific timing and particle counts are not documented in the text. Confidence: low.

## 4. Abilities
Bankai special (general, with ability area increase):
- **Hakka no Togame** (白霞罸 / Hakka no Togame / "Censure of the White Haze" / "White Haze Punishment") [src1] confidence: high.
  - Effect: Increases the area in which Rukia can drop temperature to absolute zero. On release, a pillar of cold mist rises from her. She raises her blade toward the target and releases a massive wave of freezing cold (wiki compares it to shikai "Tsugi no mai, Hakuren") that freezes everything in its path to absolute zero ([src1], anime ep. 385). Confidence: med for wave (cited to anime ep. 385 only).
  - Visuals: white/clear ice, pale cold mist (estimate `#EAF8FF`), white pillar on release (manga 569). Scale: wave covers an area; no metre values given. Duration: not given. Sound: not documented.
  - Command phrase spoken by user: none confirmed in the wiki (see section 1 and section 8).
  - First appearance: manga ch. 569 (activation), anime ep. 385 ("The White Haze", TYBW). Confidence: high.

Passive / contact freezing (true power of Sode no Shirayuki):
- **Unnamed body-temperature freezing (passive contact aura)** [src1] confidence: high.
  - Effect: the wiki says the sword does not spread cold from its blade. The wielder's body temperature is brought below freezing, so anything the wielder touches freezes. The sword only extends her reach. In bankai, physical contact with Rukia causes others to begin freezing solid ([src1], ch. 568 p. 13; ch. 570 p. 4-5; anime ep. 385). Confidence: high.
  - Visuals: frost/ice spread on touched objects (inferred from the freezing-on-touch description; no visual value given). Scale: touch range. Duration: continuous while she is in the state.
  - Command phrase: none.
  - First appearance: the true power is shown in manga ch. 567 pp. 1-16 (freezing the thorns on touch), with bankai contact effect in ch. 570. Confidence: high.

Absolute zero (self-cryostasis and area freeze):
- **Absolute zero / cryostasis (self)** [src1] confidence: high.
  - Effect: she can lower her body temperature until the ground under her freezes instantly ("ice quake") and reach absolute zero. Only about 4 seconds at absolute zero is safe ([src1], ch. 567 p. 13-18). In cryostasis she stops her molecules, which blocks substances from entering her body; optical attacks still work because her nerves keep running ([src1], ch. 568 p. 13). Confidence: high.
  - Visuals: ice quake (ground), cold mist; scale: local area.
  - First appearance: manga ch. 567 (pp. 13-18), anime ep. 385.

Shikai technique used as the active area freeze baseline (for comparison; shikai, not bankai):
- **Some no mai, Tsukishiro** (初の舞・月白 / First Dance, Moon White / "Dance Number One, White Moon") [src1] confidence: high.
  - Effect: she draws a circle with the tip while holding the sword upside down, freezing the space inside the circle and everything in it, including air above. The victim shatters along with the ice ([src1], ch. 201 p. 17-23, anime ep. 117).
  - Visuals: an extending pillar of light that freezes everything in the circle. Shape: circle, pillar. Scale: the circle. Duration: not stated.
  - Command phrase: "First Dance / Dance Number One" (name called out) [src1]. Confidence: high.
  - First appearance: ch. 201 (technique), anime ep. 117. Confidence: high.
  - Note: the wiki does not describe an "ice flower" appearing on the target. Treat the "ice flower on target" from the brief as unverified.

Other ice-related (for context):
- Third Dance, White Sword ("San no mai, Shirafune") forms a blade of ice ([src1], ch. 268; anime ep. 197). Confidence: high. Not bankai.

## 5. Anime vs manga differences
- Anime ep. 385 ("The White Haze", TYBW, aired 2023-08-12) is the anime depiction of the bankai in the same arc as manga ch. 567-570 ([src6](https://daddyjim.ai/ja/bleach/episode/The-White-Haze-episode), [src7](https://times.abema.tv/articles/-/10091386)). Confidence: high.
- Anime-visible (cited only to anime ep. 385 in the wiki): the massive freezing wave toward the target, and contact freezing of other people. Mark as anime-only detail, confidence: med.
- Manga-only as cited: release pillar (ch. 569 p. 16), crack on back of hand (ch. 569), Ōken clothing destruction (ch. 570 p. 7), time limit and thaw dialogue (ch. 570 p. 4-10). Mark as manga-first, confidence: med (anime may show the same).
- Game-only (not canon): Brave Souls release line and game forms; Jump Force "Awakening Super" cinematic; Fortnite skin. These are not anime or manga.
- Anime visual differences beyond these were not documented in text. Anime-only filler effects: none found for bankai.

## 8. Open questions / uncertainty
- Canon spoken release phrase: not found in the wiki text. Only the Brave Souls game line "Bankai! Hakka no Togame!" is documented. Need the anime ep. 385 subtitles or the manga text of ch. 569 to confirm whether she says a phrase or only "Bankai".
- Blade length and tsuba/hilt for the bankai state: not documented. All length values are estimates (low).
- HEX colours: visual estimates from rukia_bankai_2, _3 and _5 (eyeballed, not pixel-sampled). Pixel-sample before final use.
- Anime-only details (wave, contact freeze): cited only to ep. 385 in the wiki. Confirm with the episode. Timestamps not known.
- Ice flower on target (from brief): not described in the wiki. Only the shikai "shatter" effect and bankai chest flower are documented.
- Name variants: "Hakka no Togame" is consistent in the wiki and the search results. Watch for a "Hakka no Togane" typo and the unofficial "白霞の祟り" form.
- Ice / absolute-zero numbers: "safe for no more than 4 seconds" is from the wiki (ch. 567). Confirm with the manga page if this matters for gameplay tuning.
- Wiki article HTML was blocked (402/403). Facts came from the MediaWiki API wikitext. The chapter numbers in the brief (568-570) are correct for the release, but the absolute-zero explanation starts earlier (ch. 567 pp. 13-18).
- Dubs: English dub names for the zanpakuto and bankai were not verified.
- Fortnite skin, Jump Force and Brave Souls are not canon. Do not use them as references for the official look.
