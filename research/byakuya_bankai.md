## 2. Appearance (per state)

### Sealed Zanpakuto (reference for the Bankai release)
- Overall length: not stated by sources. Est. about 1.0-1.1 m, katana-shaped. confidence: low (est.).
- Blade: F says the sealed form "resembles a typical katana". Shape: long, slim, single-edged, slightly curved. Length not given.
- Tsuba (guard): bronze, open frame "akin to a four-pane window". [F](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Byakuya_Kuchiki) confidence: high.
- Tsuka (hilt) and tsuka-ito (wrap): bronze-coloured hilt, wrapped in lavender cord. est. #B7A3D1 (lavender cord) and #A67C3D (bronze fittings). Source: F. confidence: high (description), med (hex).
- Saya (scabbard): white. est. #F2F2EE. confidence: high (description), med (hex).
- Kashira, menuki: not described in sources checked.
- Ribbons, tassels: none noted.

### Bankai: Senbonzakura Kageyoshi (released form)
- Overall shape: the sealed blade fragments into two rows of giant blades (the "giant blades" are the released Bankai blades). Blade shape: long, slim, pointed tip, slight curve, katana-like. confidence: med (images).
- Giant blade size: not stated by sources. From reference frames (image 3, Byakuya in the centre of two rows), each giant blade looks several times taller than Byakuya. My estimate is about 5-10 m (not measured). confidence: low (est.).
- Number of giant blades in the initial rows: F says two rows of a thousand giant blades rise (ch.142 release, see section 3). confidence: med-high.
- Count of petal-blades after the scatter: F gives "a hundred million" blades (ch.301 p.20). JW says "said to be hundreds of millions". W says "millions". confidence: med (number conflicts; see section 8).
- Colour of the giant blades: no colour stated. est. silver-white with grey shading: #DDE2EA (highlight), #9AA3B4 (shade). Ref: image 3 (anime, ep.52) and image 4 (anime, ep.58). confidence: low (est.).
- Colour of the petal-blades (manga): est. white-lavender #E9E7F4 with shading #B9B6D6 on a dark navy night sky #1C2540 → #2D3A66. Ref: image 2 (manga, ch.142) and image 5 (manga, ch.161). confidence: low (est.).
- Byakuya's uniform (for scale and colour): haori est. #F5F5F0; hakama est. #151515; scarf est. #EDEFF5. Refs: image 1 and image 2. confidence: low (est.).
- Glow / emissive parts: the Bankai blades themselves are not described as glowing in the released form. The glow appears in Senkei (section 4). confidence: med.

## 3. Transition between states

Release sequence (manga ch.142 is the basis; F ref: ch.142 pp.12-15; also F ch.472 p.6 for the later version):
1. Byakuya drops the sealed sword straight down. The blade does not need to be intact; he can drop broken pieces together. [F, ch.472 p.6]
2. The blade sinks into the ground as if it vanished into water, with a ripple spreading out.
3. The landscape darkens. Within seconds, two rows of giant blades rise from the ground, one to the left and one to the right of Byakuya.
4. The giant blades scatter into innumerable tiny blades (the petal-blades).
5. Deactivation reconstitutes the sword in his hand in its sealed form. [F, ch.302 p.5]

Timing: F says "within seconds". est. 2-4 s from drop to full storm. confidence: low (est.).
Light/particles: the landscape darkens and the petal storm is the main particle effect. Sound character: not described in sources checked.

Anime vs manga:
- Manga: the drop is shown in close pose (image 1). The release is drawn as a dark-navy field with white petal-blades (image 2).
- Anime (ep.52 first Bankai showing, F gallery): the giant blades form a converging V-shape around Byakuya on a blue ground (image 3).
- Anime ep.58: the release is shown again against Ichigo with the blades forming a curved wall (image 4).

## 4. Abilities

### 4.1 Bankai release (Senbonzakura Kageyoshi, base form)
- **Name:** 千本桜景厳 / Senbonzakura Kageyoshi / sub: see section 1 / dub: not verified. [F](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Byakuya_Kuchiki). confidence: high.
- **Effect (own words):** The Bankai blades are a much larger version of the Shikai. They can be guided mentally, and Byakuya's hand movements double their speed. They can attack in large waves and also form a defensive wall around him. Before his training with the Royal Guard, the blade count was stated as about a hundred million.
- **Visuals:** blades silver-white (est. #DDE2EA), petals white-lavender on navy (est. #E9E7F4 / #1C2540), scale: giant blades several times Byakuya's height (est.). Duration: not stated.
- **Command phrase:** "Scatter" (chire) (see section 1).
- **First appearance:** manga ch.142 (release shown on Renji, F ch.142 pp.12-15; F gallery "142Byakuya drops" and "142Byakuya's Bankai, Senbonzakura Kageyoshi"); anime ep.52 "Renji, Oath of the Soul! Death Match with Byakuya" (W episode list; F gallery "Ep52SenbonzakuraKageyoshi"). confidence: high (manga), med-high (anime ep.52).

### 4.2 Gōkei / 吭景 ("Mawscape" in F; "Throat Scene" in F's viz field; W: "Pivotal Scape")
- **Effect:** The blades form a spherical formation around the target, sweep in from all sides with no gap, and then collapse inward on the target. It can destroy a large building from the inside. Byakuya had to move away with Shunpo from the shockwave.
- **Visuals:** sphere of pink-magenta blades (est. #F59AD6 with white core #FFFFFF), shockwave (est. #F080F0 / #C040C0). Ref: image 6 (anime ep.198, tower destroyed). Scale: not stated by sources.
- **Command phrase:** none found in sources; the technique name is spoken.
- **First appearance:** manga ch.301 "King of the Kill" p.22 (F); anime ep.198 (F). confidence: med-high.

### 4.3 Senkei / 殲景 ("Slaughterscape" in F; "Annihilation Scape" in W; viz "Annihilate")
- **Effect:** The scattered blades are compressed into roughly a thousand glowing swords. They enclose Byakuya and his opponent in a rotating structure of four rows, which is a pure offensive form (no defence). Byakuya calls the swords into his hand for melee. Requires Byakuya's blood to activate (F). Once in hand the swords lose their glow.
- **After training:** the structure becomes six rows with two extra sets of six behind them; he can use the swords together (F, ch.671 pp.15-16, anime ep.410).
- **Visuals:** pink-glowing vertical blades in rows (est. #F25FB8 with core #FFD3EE), dark violet background (est. #2A1433). Ref: image 7 (anime ep.59). confidence: low (est. colours).
- **First appearance:** manga ch.143 pp.10-12 (partial swords), ch.144 pp.1-3 (full Senkei, Ichigo as only the second witness); W cites ch.164 p.11 for a related remark; anime ep.59 (F). confidence: med-high.

### 4.4 Ikka Senjinka / 一咬千刃花 ("Flower of a Thousand Blades in a Single Bite"; viz "One Bite Thousand Blade Flowers"; W lists it under the same English as Senkei, likely an error)
- **Effect:** Byakuya raises his left hand; all the Senkei swords turn and launch in unison at the target from all sides. It is Senkei's ultimate attack and can reduce a target's head to dust. Used on Gerard Valkyrie in his Godly Size and Vollständig form.
- **Visuals:** a barrage of pink lights (F), rendered as white-blue rays in manga (image 8) and a pink burst in anime (image 9, est. #FFB0E6 core, #F060C8 glow). Scale: not stated.
- **Command phrase:** the technique name is spoken.
- **First appearance:** manga ch.671 pp.16-17 (F); anime ep.410 (F). confidence: med-high.

### 4.5 Shūkei: Hakuteiken / 終景・白帝剣 ("Endscape: White Emperor Sword" (F); "Final Scape" (W); viz "Last Sight: White Emperor Sword")
- **Effect:** All Senkei swords are condensed into one sword with greatly increased offensive power. Huge spiritual energy forms large white wings and a vertical halo on Byakuya's back, with a bird-like aura shape. The blade shines bright white. Used on Ichigo in the Soul Society arc. It inflicted severe wounds in one blow.
- **Visuals:** white wings (est. #F4F8FF), halo (white), blade (white); manga background dark navy (est. #1A2750) with sand ground (est. #B49C7A). Ref: image 11 (manga ch.166), image 10 (anime ep.59).
- **Command phrase:** the technique name is spoken.
- **First appearance:** manga ch.166 pp.16-19 (F, W); anime ep.59 (F). Note: anime ep.252 also shows it on Kōga (F gallery "Byakuya Kuchiki Shūkei Hakuteiken (ep252)"). confidence: high (manga ch.166), med (anime ep.59 number, see section 8).

### 4.6 Hand control (manual steering of the Bankai blades)
- **Effect:** Byakuya controls the Bankai blades mentally, and moving his hands doubles their speed. This is the "hand-control" / "Yoshi" element in the brief (see section 8: no separate "Yoshi" technique name was found).
- **Visuals:** blades rendered as white streaks, hand extended (image 12, manga ch.163 p.17).
- **First appearance:** manga ch.163 p.17 (F). Anime ep.58 shows the same gesture (F gallery "Ep58ByakuyaUsesHands"). confidence: high (manga), med (anime ep.58).

### 4.7 Defensive wall / sphere
- **Effect:** The Bankai blades form a defensive wall that can block physical attacks and energy blasts. They can fully enclose Byakuya to protect him from shockwaves. Other spells are used for defence (see Dankū below).
- **Visuals:** curved blade-wall around Byakuya (image 4, anime ep.58, est. blade #AEB6C5 with highlights #F0F3F8, sky #1A2E66).
- **First appearance:** manga ch.161 pp.12-16 and ch.163 p.10; anime ep.58 (F). confidence: med-high.

### 4.8 Hurtless Area / Mushōken (無傷圏, viz "Safe Zone") [passive named ability]
- **Effect:** The blades do not strike anything within about 85 cm of Byakuya unless he lets them. It protects him from his own Bankai.
- **Visuals:** not a visible effect; shown in anime ep.364 (F, image not downloaded).
- **First appearance:** manga ch.469 pp.5-6 and ch.472 pp.11-13 (F). anime ep.364 (F). confidence: high.

### 4.9 Dankū (断空, "Splitting Void") [not a Bankai technique]
- **Effect:** A Bakudō #81 defensive barrier. Byakuya and others use it. F lists it as a Kidō spell, not a Bankai technique. Listed here only because the brief asks for defensive Danku-like uses.
- **Visuals:** translucent rectangular wall (F gallery, ep.198 and ep.252 Dankū images; not downloaded).
- **First appearance:** anime ep.198 (F gallery). confidence: med.

### 4.10 Other names checked
- Senka: a non-Bankai technique used by Byakuya (F gallery, ep.17 and ep.52). Kanji not verified. Not part of the Bankai.
- Utsusemi: not part of the Bankai.
- No other Bankai-specific names found in the sources checked.

## 5. Anime vs manga differences

- Petal colour: manga petals read as white-lavender on navy (images 2, 5). Anime frames show the blades as silver-white blades on blue (images 3, 4). The anime also shows Senkei blades in pink (image 7). confidence: med (visual, from frames).
- Senkei glow: the anime (ep.59) shows pink flames from Byakuya's blood (F gallery "Ep59ByakuyaFlamingBlood"). The manga has no equivalent pink flame frame in the gallery. anime-only visual (per F gallery, not confirmed by manga reading). confidence: med.
- Ikka Senjinka colour: manga (ch.671) is white-blue rays; anime (ep.410) is a pink burst. Visual difference, same technique. confidence: med.
- Hakuteiken: manga ch.166 and anime ep.59 both show white wings and a halo. JW says the anime version of the reiatsu takes a bird-like beast shape. confidence: med.
- Blade count: manga ch.301 says a hundred million blades before the Royal Guard training. Post-training, the number increased (F). No exact post-training count given. confidence: med.
- Anime-only: Dankū (Kidō) appears in anime ep.198/252 (F gallery), not a manga Bankai technique. anime-only (for the Bankai context). confidence: med.
- Anime-only filler-like content: none identified for the Bankai in sources checked.

## 8. Open questions / uncertainty

1. Petal count: F says a hundred million blades (ch.301 p.20). JW says hundreds of millions. W says millions. Unresolved.
2. Bankai kanji reading: JW gives the romaji as "Kagemitsu" while F and W use "Kageyoshi". Using Kageyoshi (high confidence in F and W). Check the official English release if needed.
3. Bankai translation: F "Vibrant Display of a Thousand Cherry Blossoms" vs W "Vibrant Scape of a Thousand Cherry Blossoms". The English sub is unresolved; both are translations.
4. English dub names: no dub source was checked. All dub fields are "not verified". The English dub names of Gōkei/Senkei/Ikka/Hakuteiken are unknown.
5. Gōkei English sub: F says "Mawscape" (viz "Throat Scene"), W says "Pivotal Scape". Unresolved.
6. Ikka Senjinka English sub: W lists "Annihilation Scape" (same as Senkei), which looks like an error. F gives "Flower of a Thousand Blades in a Single Bite". Use F.
7. Hakuteiken anime episode: F cites ep.59. A search snippet suggested "Conclusion of the Death Match" might be ep.62. Unconfirmed; F's gallery and refs point to ep.59.
8. Senkei first appearance: F cites ch.144 and ep.59. W cites ch.164 p.11 for a related remark. The very first full Senkei moment is ch.144 (per F).
9. Senkei in ep.52: F's Senkei anime gallery includes "Ep52ByakuyaDefeatsRenji" (Byakuya pins Renji with swords). It is not confirmed whether that is Senkei or plain Bankai blades.
10. "Yoshi" in the brief: no technique named "Yoshi" was found. The hand control (section 4.6) is the closest match. Likely a shorthand for Kageyoshi.
11. Giant-blade size: no source gives a metric size. The 5-10 m estimate is mine, from frame proportions (low confidence).
12. Hex colours: all colour values are my estimates from the downloaded frames, not from sources. Anime and manga palettes differ.
13. Shikai vs Bankai release: the Bankai has no separate release phrase. Both use "Scatter".
14. The image 1 (ch.142 "drops") file and image 12 (ch.163 hand-direct) are manga. Their colour/scale is not verified against an anime frame.
15. Bleach Fandom was reached through the MediaWiki API (tools/fandom.py). Some Fandom page URLs (HTML) return 403 to scripts; the wiki text was read via API.
