## 2. Appearance (per state)

### 2a. SEALED
- Overall length: **not given** on the wiki, ja.wiki, or any other source. Not measured. A product-listing replica quotes a 27 inch blade, but that is merchandise (low): [Syncee](https://syncee.com/product/437886_74012_6444250566/rukia-kuchiki-anime-sword).
- Blade shape: "a normal katana" ([wiki Zanpakutō](https://bleach.fandom.com/wiki/Rukia_Kuchiki), high). No curvature given.
- Blade surface: a series of curved lines embossed on either side of the blade (wiki, high).
- Tsuba (guard): rectangular. A vaguely flame-like pattern on the two long sides, a curved line on the short sides, inward-curved corners with curved slits above and below the blade (wiki, high).
- Tsuka (hilt): reddish-brown (wiki, high). Image check (rukia_sealed_1, ch. 135 close-up): the wrap reads as deep red with a diamond pattern and the guard as tan/gold (see colours below). This differs from "reddish-brown" in tone and adds a diamond wrap pattern.
- Tsuka-ito (wrap): deep red with a diamond-shaped pattern in the ch. 135 close-up (image-based, med). The wiki does not describe the wrap.
- Kashira / menuki: not visible in sampled frames; not described. Unknown.
- Saya (scabbard): dark, visible on the floor in ep. 1 (rukia_sealed_7); no colour or detail recorded. Unknown beyond "dark" (low).
- Ribbons: none in the sealed form (wiki describes the ribbon only in shikai, high).
- Colours (sampled from images; estimates):
  - Tsuka-ito red: `#822933` (sampled, rukia_sealed_1, dominant red pixels).
  - Tsuba tan/gold: `#AF9668` (sampled, rukia_sealed_1, guard).
  - Blade steel: `#D1D9E8` (sampled, rukia_sealed_6, lit blade; highlights bias it light).
- Glow / emissive: none.

## 3. Transition between states
- Trigger: Rukia holds the sword out in front of her and turns it in a counter-clockwise circle while calling "Dance" (舞え) (wiki, high).
- Visual change: blade, hilt and tsuba turn completely white; the tsuba becomes a hollow snowflake-like circle; a white ribbon forms from the pommel (wiki, high).
- Accompaniment: a rush of cold air in all directions (wiki, high).
- Duration, light, sound effect: **not given** on the wiki. Not measured.
- Manga vs anime: the release is in manga ch. 201 (p.16) and anime ep. 117 (wiki, high). Ch. 202 (p.3) and ep. 117 describe the shikai as the most beautiful zanpakuto in Soul Society (wiki, high).

## 4. Abilities (SHIKAI)

Shikai "dances" are all ice-based. The wiki also describes a general ice ability: Rukia's body temperature drops below freezing, so anything she touches freezes (wiki, high).

### 4.1 Some no mai, Tsukishiro (初の舞・月白)
- English: "First Dance, Moon White" (wiki); VIZ "Dance Number One, White Moon".
- Effect (own words): Rukia holds the blade upside down, calls the name, and slashes so that the tip draws a circle. Everything inside the circle freezes, and a column of light rises through the air to freeze everything above the circle. The target shatters soon after (wiki, high). The spirit's version can place the circle on walls behind enemies (wiki spirit page, med; not Rukia's own version).
- Visuals: a light pillar (described as "extending pillar of light") freezing the air above; a ring-shaped freeze on the ground. Colour: light-blue/white (sampled). Scale: reaches the sky (no metres). Duration: not given.
- Command phrase: "Dance", then the dance name (wiki, high).
- **First appearance: manga ch. 201 p.17-23; anime ep. 117** (wiki, high). The ep. 154 claim from daddyjim is not supported: the wiki cites ep. 154 only as a reference where the ice pillar does not shatter a victim outside it.

### 4.2 Tsugi no mai, Hakuren (次の舞・白漣)
- English: "Next Dance, White Ripple" (wiki); VIZ "Next Dance, White Wave".
- Effect (own words): Rukia punctures the ground once (a large ice circle forms), then punctures four times in a semicircle in front of her. Ice particles rise from the holes; she takes a battle stance, gathers the particles at the tip, and releases a large avalanche of cold air. Anything it touches flash-freezes (wiki, high).
- Visuals: ice particles, avalanche of cold air. Colours: white-blue (estimate `#C0D3E7`, low). Scale: not given. Duration: not given.
- Command phrase: "Dance" + name.
- **First appearance: manga ch. 235 p.14-17; anime ep. 140** (wiki, high). Anime ep. 149 gallery shot shows Rukia preparing it (file name only; not verified).

### 4.3 San no mai, Shirafune (参の舞・白刀)
- English: "Third Dance, White Sword" (wiki); VIZ "Dance Number Three, White Sword".
- Effect (own words): moisture in the air gathers at the tip and forms an ice blade. The sword's length can be extended, and the wiki also says Shirafune can repair the blade if it is broken. After piercing a target, it keeps freezing objects and the area around it (wiki, high).
- Visuals: extended ice blade; ice particles. Colours: not given beyond the white-blue ice effect. Scale: not given.
- Condition: the wiki says it can repair a broken blade. The "only when broken" idea (fictionhorizon) is not in the wiki and is treated as unsupported (low).
- **First appearance: manga ch. 268 p.14-17; anime ep. 160** (wiki Shirafune page, high). Later use: ch. 300 p.1-11, ep. 197 (wiki, high).

### 4.4 Juhaku (樹白, じゅはく, "Tree White")
- Listed by the wiki as a Zanpakutō technique (high).
- Effect (own words): Rukia stabs the ground. A trail of ice runs toward the target and small ice particles fill the air. The target freezes from the base upward. Frozen parts break or shatter easily (wiki, high).
- Visuals: a long ice trail across the ground, frozen tree roots; ice particles; scale and duration not given.
- **First appearance: anime ep. 272** (wiki Juhaku page gallery, ja.wiki, high). **Anime-only in the sense that Rukia never uses it in the manga** (wiki trivia: the manga shows the same frozen effect on the opponent, but not the technique). Opponent: Rudbornn Chelute (the ja.wiki transliteration "Ludbourne" refers to the same character, med).
- fictionhorizon's "impale, then shatter" version is not in the wiki (low; treated as unsupported).

### 4.5 Ice-Rope Connection
- Wiki (high): if Rukia cannot reach her sword, she creates an ice extension from her hand to the hilt. This lets her use the sword's shikai abilities at a distance.
- **First appearance: manga ch. 471 p.14-15; anime ep. 364** (wiki, high). Kanji not given (氷縄 unverified).
- Visuals: a visible ice rope/bridge from hand to hilt (wiki gallery file "IceRopeConnection.gif"). Colours and scale: not given.

## 5. Anime vs manga differences
- **anime-only**: spirit arcs "Zanpakutō Unknown Tales" and "Beast Swords" (wiki, high). Not canon per the wiki.
- **anime-only**: ep. 232 "Sode no Shirayuki vs. Rukia! Delusion of the Heart" (spirit cuts Rukia off from the shikai; Ichigo fights in Bankai; Rukia binds the spirit with kido and frees it with Sōren Sōkatsui) (daddyjim, high for existence).
- **anime-only**: the spirit's anime debut is ep. 230 (wiki spirit page, high).
- **anime-only** technique use: Juhaku is first shown in anime ep. 272. The manga shows the effect with the technique unnamed (wiki trivia, high).
- Same in both: Tsukishiro (ch. 201 / ep. 117), Hakuren (ch. 235 / ep. 140), Shirafune (ch. 268 / ep. 160), Ice-Rope (ch. 471 / ep. 364) (wiki, high).
- Colours and designs: no manga-vs-anime colour difference found in the wiki text. Image frames suggest the same design in both.
- Game-only (not anime or manga): "Hyōha Setsugekka" (氷破雪月花), a precursor to Tsukishiro in Heat the Soul 3 (wiki, high). Not for VFX unless wanted.
